    Verified artifacts
    Checks validate process exit, result JSON, artifact hashes and cleanup. They do not prove every semantic claim made by the model.
    

    Safety & limits
    Authority stays outside the agent. Only the trusted worker creates and destroys environments.
    Isolation
    gVisor, non-root, read-only root, no host mounts or Docker socket.
    Network
    Disabled. Cloud and inference credentials never enter tasks.
    Resources
    1 vCPU ceiling, up to 1024 MiB memory, 64 PIDs, 64 MiB scratch, 64 KiB output, at most 120 seconds execution.
    Artifacts
    Regular files only. 1 MiB per file, 4 MiB total, eight files maximum.
    Cleanup
    Containers are destroyed. Leases remain reserved until cleanup is verified.
    Optional profiles
    Nested Docker, browser and desktop are unavailable until separately qualified.
    Evidence
    Worker-collected records, not independent remote attestation or a security audit.
